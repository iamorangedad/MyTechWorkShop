## 六年级（Grade 6）· 9 个单元 · 43 个 Section

### 第1单元 面积和表面积（Unit 1: Area and Surface Area）

#### A部分 用推理求面积（Section A: Reasoning to Find Area）
- 学习目标：Find the area of a two-dimensional region with straight boundaries by decomposing, rearranging, subtracting, or enclosing shapes, and explain the solution method.
- 课：6.1.1 Tiling the Plane；6.1.2 Finding Area by Decomposing and Rearranging；6.1.3 Reasoning to Find Area
- 海报要点：分割、重组、割补，图形完全重合面积相等，面积可以相加

#### B部分 平行四边形（Section B: Parallelograms）
- 学习目标：Apply the formula for area of a parallelogram to find the area, the length of a base, or the height.
- 学习目标：Understand bases and heights in a parallelogram and recognize base-height pairs to use to calculate its area.
- 课：6.1.4 Parallelograms；6.1.5 Bases and Heights of Parallelograms；6.1.6 Area of Parallelograms
- 海报要点：底和对应的高，面积 = 底 × 高

#### C部分 三角形和其他多边形（Section C: Triangles and Other Polygons）
- 学习目标：Find the area of a polygon by decomposing it into parallelograms and triangles.
- 学习目标：Understand bases and heights in a triangle and recognize base-height pairs to use to find the area of a triangle.
- 学习目标：Understand why the process of finding the area of a triangle can be abstracted as 1/2 · b · h (or equivalent) and apply the formula to find the area of a triangle.
- 课：6.1.7 From Parallelograms to Triangles；6.1.8 Area of Triangles；6.1.9 Formula for the Area of a Triangle；6.1.10 Bases and Heights of Triangles；6.1.11 Polygons
- 海报要点：三角形是平行四边形的一半，面积 = ½ × 底 × 高；多边形分割成三角形

#### D部分 表面积（Section D: Surface Area）
- 学习目标：Identify or create a net that represents a prism or pyramid.
- 学习目标：Use a net to calculate the surface area of a prism or pyramid and explain the solution method.
- 课：6.1.12 What Is Surface Area?；6.1.13 Polyhedra；6.1.14 Nets and Surface Area；6.1.15 More Nets, More Surface Area；6.1.16 Distinguishing Between Surface Area and Volume (Optional)
- 海报要点：多面体、展开图，用展开图求棱柱、棱锥的表面积

#### E部分 正方形和正方体（Section E: Squares and Cubes）
- 学习目标：Interpret and write expressions with exponents 2 and 3 to represent the area of a square or the volume of a cube.
- 学习目标：Write expressions, with or without exponents, to represent the surface area of a given cube.
- 课：6.1.17 Squares and Cubes；6.1.18 Surface Area of a Cube
- 海报要点：用指数 ² 和 ³ 表示正方形面积、正方体体积；正方体表面积 6s²

#### F部分 综合运用：帐篷（Section F: Let’s Put It to Work）

- 海报要点：估算、计算做帐篷需要多少布料

### 第2单元 认识比（Unit 2: Introducing Ratios）

#### A部分 什么是比（Section A: What Are Ratios?）
- 学习目标：Create and interpret discrete diagrams that represent situations involving ratios.
- 学习目标：Create and interpret sentences that describe ratios.
- 课：6.2.1 Introducing Ratios and Ratio Language；6.2.2 Representing Ratios with Diagrams
- 海报要点：用比的语言描述两个量，用离散图表示比

#### B部分 等值比（Section B: Equivalent Ratios）
- 学习目标：Explain equivalent ratios in terms of the quantities in a recipe being multiplied by the same number to create a different size batch of something with the same taste or same shade.
- 学习目标：Generate equivalent ratios and justify that they are equivalent.
- 课：6.2.3 Recipes；6.2.4 Color Mixtures；6.2.5 Defining Equivalent Ratios
- 海报要点：食谱、调色：各部分同乘一个数，味道、颜色不变

#### C部分 表示等值比（Section C: Representing Equivalent Ratios）
- 学习目标：Given prices and quantities or distances and times, calculate the price for 1 object or the distance traveled in 1 unit of time. Express each using the word “per.”
- 学习目标：Justify whether two situations do or do not happen at the same rate, by determining if the ratios in the situations are equivalent.
- 学习目标：Use a double number line diagram to represent and find equivalent ratios.
- 课：6.2.6 Introducing Double Number Line Diagrams；6.2.7 Creating Double Number Line Diagrams；6.2.8 How Much for One?；6.2.9 Constant Speed；6.2.10 Comparing Situations by Examining Ratios
- 海报要点：双数线；每 1 份是多少（单价、速度）；比较两种情况是否同样快

#### D部分 解比和比率问题（Section D: Solving Ratio and Rate Problems）
- 学习目标：Choose multipliers strategically when solving multi-step problems involving equivalent ratios.
- 学习目标：Use a table of equivalent ratios to solve problems.
- 课：6.2.11 Representing Ratios with Tables；6.2.12 Navigating a Table of Equivalent Ratios；6.2.13 Tables and Double Number Line Diagrams；6.2.14 Solving Equivalent Ratio Problems
- 海报要点：比率表，策略性地选择乘数

#### E部分 部分与整体的比（Section E: Part-Part-Whole Ratios）
- 学习目标：Choose representations and solution methods to reason about ratios and sums of quantities.
- 学习目标：Use diagrams or other strategies to solve problems involving ratios and the total amount.
- 课：6.2.15 Part-Part-Whole Ratios；6.2.16 Solving More Ratio Problems
- 海报要点：部分 : 部分 : 整体，已知总量求各部分

#### F部分 综合运用：费米问题（Section F: Let’s Put It to Work）
- 说明（官方未列 Section Goals）：In the final section, students have the opportunity to apply their thinking from throughout the unit. As this is a short section followed by an End-of-Unit Assessment, there are no section goals or checkpoint questions.
- 课：6.2.17 A Fermi Problem
- 海报要点：用比和估算解决费米问题

### 第3单元 单位比率和百分数（Unit 3: Unit Rates and Percentages）

#### A部分 测量单位与换算（Section A: Units of Measurement and Unit Conversion）
- 学习目标：Recognize that when we measure things in two different units, the pairs of measurements are equivalent ratios. Apply this understanding to convert a measurement from one unit to another unit.
- 学习目标：Use a “rate per 1” to solve problems involving unit conversion.

- 海报要点：同一个量用两种单位测，测量值成等值比；用「每 1」换算

#### B部分 比率（Section B: Rates）

- 海报要点：一个比对应两个单位比率；比较速度和价格
- 本地化 / 安全：原书价格用美元，本海报改用人民币。

#### C部分 百分数（Section C: Percentages）
- 学习目标：Choose a strategy to solve problems involving percentages and explain the solution method.
- 学习目标：Create tape diagrams, double number line diagrams, or tables to represent situations involving percentages.
- 课：6.3.10 What Are Percentages?；6.3.11 Representing Percentages with Double Number Line Diagrams；6.3.12 Representing Percentages in Different Ways；6.3.13 Benchmark Percentages；6.3.14 Solving Percentage Problems；6.3.15 Finding This Percent of That；6.3.16 Finding the Percentage (Optional)
- 海报要点：百分数是每 100 中的几；用双数线、线段图、表格解百分数问题

#### D部分 综合运用：粉刷房间（Section D: Let’s Put It to Work）
